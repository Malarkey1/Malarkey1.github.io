Daniel Rutman portfolio, DS edition: open index.html to start.
Keep every file and the images folder together.

  index.html, about.html, experience.html, resume.html, contact.html   the pages
  style.css      colours and fonts (top of the file). All sizes are rem-based, so the UI scales up on big screens
  script.js      your name, the four "games" and the cartridge pictures (top of the file) plus all behaviour
  images/        pictures for the spinning cartridge (placeholders: replace the files or edit PICS in script.js)
  mii-*.png      your Mii, cut into body, upper arms and forearms so the elbows can bend
  stylus.svg     the stylus used by the "tap here" hint
  resume.pdf     placeholder, replace with your own resume (same file name)

Dark mode switches on automatically from 6pm to 6am; the sun/moon button in the status bar overrides it.
On wide screens the grey side margins hold silhouettes (game dev on the left, hardware and IT on the right)
and work as a doodle pad: left-click draws, right-click erases. The tools hide in a strip that pokes out of the
bottom-right corner (hover the pencil).

Your earlier designs are untouched in the site folder.
